# Sayward 24-hour revenue plan

The controllable objective is to put a tested $3 offer in front of relevant people quickly.
A sale is a market outcome and cannot be guaranteed or recorded until Stripe shows one.

## Hour 0: launch gate

- Complete Stripe phone verification.
- Create the live product, one-time $3 price, and Payment Link.
- Redirect successful checkout to the public Sayward URL with the return value.
- Build with the real Payment Link, rerun tests, deploy publicly, and verify the full flow.
- Record the launch time and zero-revenue baseline.

## Hours 1–3: first distribution

- Publish the short post on one existing audience channel available to the owner.
- Share in up to two relevant communities only where self-promotion is explicitly allowed.
- Send the direct-share note only to existing contacts who have already expressed interest in
  difficult-conversation or writing help.
- Use `artifacts/design/sayward-concept.png` or `public/og.png` as the visual.

## Hours 4–12: learn from behavior

- Check Stripe for payment-link views and completed payments if those metrics are available.
- If there are views but no payments, revise the offer copy before changing the product.
- If there are no views, improve distribution rather than altering the generator.
- Record observations without inventing conversion rates from tiny samples.

## Hours 12–24: one measured iteration

- Make at most one narrow change based on actual evidence: headline, price framing, or audience.
- Reverify checkout after any offer change.
- Report sales and gross revenue exactly as Stripe shows them, including zero.

## Consistency path after the first day

- Keep the free opening useful so the paid value is easy to judge.
- Add server-verified restore access only if real purchases justify the complexity.
- Build search-oriented pages for specific scenarios only after validating that one audience
  converts; do not manufacture SEO pages or testimonials.
- Review support/refund requests and script quality before expanding scenarios.
